# A normalized formula-side mixed-rank ensemble

For any central-column count `p`, positive-noncentral-column count `L` and natural `q`, ordered positive noncentral parameters define a normalized probability measure on `Fin (p+L) → ℝ`. Its density relative to the product of positive-half-line Lebesgue measures is a product of two determinants, extended by zero outside the positive half-line. Its count probabilities for an arbitrary measurable set are given by the existing determinant-coefficient formula. The construction includes `p = 0`, `L = 0`, and empty total dimension. It is an explicit candidate ensemble, not an identification of the actual noncentral Wishart joint law or the original paper's CDF formulas.

The mixed series numerator is invariant under permutation of physical nodes: both determinants receive the same sign. It is positive for distinct positive nodes and zero when nodes repeat. The globally extended determinant product is nonnegative, and its positive-side expression is that numerator times positive row weights. The scalar series moment is `exp(lambda)` times the finite Poisson-factorial polynomial; each pair of ensemble basis functions is integrable against the restricted measure. The resulting moment matrix is the previously verified mixed noncentral moment matrix after a simultaneous index equivalence and positive noncentral column scaling. Its determinant is positive, allowing the already verified determinant-ensemble normalization and count theorem to apply.
