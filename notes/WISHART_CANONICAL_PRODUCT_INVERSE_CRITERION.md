The real final-source Qwen3.8-Max review flags two alleged gaps, resolved from the actual proof directions and model signature:

1. For positive f,t, inverse(f)^p <= inverse(min(f,t))^p. Therefore integrability of the larger truncated-inverse function implies integrability of the smaller original inverse. This is exactly the forward implication and mono' domination used. The reviewer itself derives the correct ordering but then incorrectly denies that integrability of the larger function implies that of the smaller. The lemma one_div_le_one_div_of_le takes positivity of the smaller denominator min(f,t) and min(f,t)<=f, and gives precisely 1/f<=1/min(f,t). The reverse implication uses the integrable sum inverse(f)^p+inverse(t)^p. Both directions have explicit AE positivity.
2. The model in actualCorrelatedRank_eq_generic_ae is Z*B+M, not B*Z+M. Here Z is a-by-b and B=I_b is b-by-b, so right multiplication is dimensionally correct for every a,b. The exact generic rank then reduces to min(a,b), since rank(I_b)=b and its kernel is zero. No requirement a=b or b<=a is used.
