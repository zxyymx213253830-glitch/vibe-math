# Candidate and actual trace Laplace transforms

For every finite complex mean matrix M, the actual proper complex Gaussian Gram trace has transform `(1+s)^(-m*n) * exp(-s*sum(abs(Mij)^2)/(1+s))` for `s>=0`, including empty dimensions. If `m=p+L+q`, `n=p+L` and the squared Frobenius norm of M equals the sum of the candidate's strictly ordered positive lambda parameters, the actual and candidate scalar trace transforms agree. The sum condition is explicit and suffices for this scalar comparison; the theorem does not identify the individual noncentral eigenvalues or any joint spectrum law.
