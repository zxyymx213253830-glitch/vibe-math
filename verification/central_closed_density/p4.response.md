## Concrete Issues

### 1. Density Constant Definition vs. Stated Normalization

`centralWishartDensityConstant` is defined as:
$$K(d,n) = \prod_{j=0}^{n-1} \pi^{-j} \cdot ((d+j)!)^{-1}$$

The docstring claims normalization is "$\pi^{-n(n-1)/2}$ divided by the factorial product." This is **correct**: $\sum_{j=0}^{n-1} j = n(n-1)/2$. However, the density itself (`centralWishartCoordinateDensity`) multiplies $K(d,n) \cdot (\det H)^d \cdot e^{-\operatorname{tr} H}$ without any additional $\pi$ power. The actual CN$(0,1)$ density on $\mathbb{C}^n$ carries $\pi^{-n} e^{-\|z\|^2}$, and the induction in `centralWishartCoordinateDensity_bartlett` accumulates exactly $\pi^{-n}$ per step via `(Real.pi⁻¹) ^ n`. This is consistent—no missing factor—but the relationship between $K(d,s)$ and the full normalizing constant of the Wishart density (which classically includes $\pi^{-s(s-1)/2}$ from off-diagonal integration) is **not independently verified**; it emerges purely from the recursion. This is acceptable but leaves no explicit checkpoint confirming $K(d,s)$ matches the known closed-form Wishart normalization $\frac{\pi^{-s(s-1)/2}}{\prod_{j=0}^{s-1}(d+j)!}$.

### 3. Row Surplus Parameter Stability

At induction step $n$ (building column $n+1$), the leading entry has $t - n = d + n + 1 - 1 = d + n$ complex degrees of freedom, i.e., Gamma$(d+n+1, 1)$ in the **real** parameterization used here. This is **consistent**.
