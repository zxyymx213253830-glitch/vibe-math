## P4 Adversarial Review: `ActualCanonicalFinBridge.lean`

**1. Mathematical gap: `eigenvalueCongr` requires equal Hermitian proofs, not just equal matrices.**
The theorem `eigenvalueCongr` takes two separate hypotheses `hG : G.IsHermitian` and `hJ : J.IsHermitian`, then substitutes `G = J`. However, `eigenvalues₀` depends on the specific Hermitian proof (via `Eigenspace`/spectral theorem construction). After `subst J`, the goal becomes `hG.eigenvalues₀ = h.eigenvalues₀` where `h : G = G`, but this does **not** follow by `rfl` unless `hG = h` propositionally. The proof `by subst J; rfl` silently assumes proof irrelevance for the Hermitian certificate, which is not generally available in Lean's type theory. This is an actual gap unless `IsHermitian` is a subsingleton (which it is, being a proposition—but the `subst` still leaves the wrong proof term on the RHS). The fix requires `heq_rec` or explicitly invoking proof irrelevance; as written, the `rfl` is unjustified.

The dependency chain requires that `canonicalSumRowAction_gram` holds for the specific product `X*F` where `X : Matrix (Fin m) (Fin p)` and `F : Matrix (Fin p) (Fin n)` (after submatrix). However, `X` is defined as `Z*B + M` inside the AE theorem but left implicit in `canonical_physical_fin_gram`—the statement quantifies over raw `Z` without establishing that `Z*B + M` matches the argument structure expected by the imported `canonicalSumRowAction_gram`. If that lemma requires structural conditions on its matrix argument beyond typing, they are unchecked here.
